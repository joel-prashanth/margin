What Margin does

The paperwork, handled. The judgement, yours.

✎

Rubric-first AI drafts

Every journal gets a score per criterion — experience, feelings, learning, application, conclusion — with the reasoning beside it.

⎙

Reads the whole PDF

Lab reports, essays and project reports are read end to end, including tables and hand-drawn diagrams.

⚡

A class in one click

Batch-grade every pending submission. Margin paces itself around AI rate limits and keeps going.

✓

Saves you can trust

After each save, Margin reads the record back from Aurora ERP and checks every score matches what you approved.

▦

Attendance from your sheet

Map each class to its Google Sheet tab once. Preview the match against the ERP roster, then submit.

◧

Better ERP pages

A live draft gradebook, Term 2-only views and load-all tables, right inside the Aurora portal.

How it works

Three steps, no new logins.

  1. 1

    Add Margin to Chrome

    It works with the Aurora ERP session you already have open. No passwords to share.

  2. 2

    Pick a class and assessment

    Margin finds your classes and the journals, labs, essays and projects waiting to be graded.

  3. 3

    Review, adjust, save

    Every AI score is a draft. Change anything, then save to Aurora ERP when you're happy.
